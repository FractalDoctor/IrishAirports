Irish Airports

Every airfield in Ireland, for iPhone and iPad

The guide to every airfield on the island of Ireland, North and South. From Dublin and Shannon to grass farm strips, microlight fields, island aerodromes, heliports and the seaplane base on Lough Derg, with the lost airfields of the past alongside them.

Features

  • 153 open airfields on one map of the island, each marked by its kind
  • Runways with length, width, surface and lighting, and radio frequencies in the order you would use them
  • Live METAR and TAF for every field that reports one, with the time each report was retrieved
  • Distance and bearing from where you stand, and dawn, sunrise, sunset and dusk at each field
  • Radio, every airfield frequency on the island and Shannon VOLMET on HF
  • Heritage, the stories of 33 lost airfields, each written from cited sources
  • A logbook of passport stamps, with a gold star for each field you verify in person
  • Poster-style cards to share a visit or your tally, and logbook export and import
  • Universal — on iPad and the open iPhone Duo the field slides in beside the map, and folding the Duo carries an open field across to the cover display
  • No accounts, no tracking, no ads
